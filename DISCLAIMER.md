# Disclaimer

The ERG Analysis API is the companion code of *Hands-On Electroretinography in the Age of AI* (Tavakoli, Apress). It is designed as a clinical decision support tool for full-field ERG recordings, as described in the book: it would support the clinical judgment of a qualified healthcare professional, not replace it, and its output is not a medical diagnosis.

**Current status: research and teaching use only.** The code and the hosted demonstration have not been validated on patients and are not a medical device. They have not been assessed, certified or registered with any regulator, and must not be used for clinical decisions about a patient. Upload anonymised or simulated recordings only. The intended use below describes the use the software is designed for, which requires external validation and regulatory approval first (`docs/regulatory/T3-D` and `T3-E`).

## Mandatory output disclaimer

Every API report carries this disclaimer (Chapter 1 §1.7.4):

> This result is generated by an automated signal analysis algorithm and is intended to support, not replace, clinical judgment. It does not constitute a medical diagnosis. All results should be interpreted in the context of the patient's full clinical history, examination findings, and relevant investigations by a qualified healthcare professional. A Green ERG signal does not exclude significant ocular or systemic pathology. If you have any concern about this patient's visual function, please refer to an appropriate specialist.

## Intended use

From the Model Card (Chapter 17 §17.2.2):

- **Use:** triage and disease classification of full-field ERG recordings, as clinical decision support.
- **Patients:** adults aged 18 to 80 years attending ophthalmology or clinical electrophysiology services.
- **Users:** retinal electrophysiologists, specialist ophthalmologists, optometrists working in extended-scope electrophysiology clinics, and clinical research teams conducting ERG-based population screening studies.
- **Review:** a qualified clinician must review every RED and AMBER Traffic Light result before any clinical action is taken.

## What the current release does and does not do

- The Traffic Light compares each parameter with the age-specific reference limits of Baker et al. (2025) and their 90% confidence intervals (Chapter 10 §10.4, Equation 10.2). The zones are GREEN, AMBER, RED and ATYPICAL DIRECTION, and the most severe zone sets the composite result.
- The limits are silver thread (DTL fiber) limits. Gold foil amplitudes are converted to silver thread equivalents first. No limits are held for contact lens or skin electrodes, so those recordings receive no zone.
- The b/a ratio, the oscillatory potentials and the PhNR have no published limits and are reported as measured values, not zoned.
- The six-class disease classifier is a pre-registered target (Chapter 19, Table 19.10). It has not been trained or validated on patient data. The performance figures in the Model Card (Chapter 17, Table 17.4) are illustrative placeholders, not results.
- The case values in `examples/` are constructed for teaching. They are not patient data.

## Safe failure

The pipeline does not produce an unreliable result silently (Chapter 19 §19.9):

- A recording whose SNR is below the Pass threshold for its electrode (Chapter 2) is reported with a signal quality caveat, and the SHAP attribution is not shown. A recording below the Fail threshold receives no Traffic Light.
- A recording with no electrode type halts before any signal processing, with an error report that names the missing field and its valid values.

## Data

The FHIR output carries the age in whole years at the recording date, never the date of birth, in line with the data minimization principle of the GDPR (Appendix B). Do not submit names, dates of birth or national identifiers in the patient ID field.
